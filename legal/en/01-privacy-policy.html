Privacy Policy — HikeTag

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Who we are

HikeTag ("we", "the app") is a mobile application and a website (www.hiketag.ro) that let users in Romania organize, discover, and join hiking trips, mountain excursions, and other outdoor events together with other users. Everything below about "the app" also applies to the website.

The data controller for HikeTag is:

Alexandru Tabara, private individual

Location: Bucharest, Romania

Data protection contact email: hiketag@gmail.com

This policy applies to anyone using the HikeTag app in Romania. The app is intended exclusively for people 16 years of age or older.

2. What data we collect

2.1 Account and profile data

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored only in encrypted form, by our authentication provider)
  • Gender (woman, man, or "Prefer not to say") — also used to show or hide events marked "women only" / "men only"
  • Year of birth — used to check the minimum age; your age is hidden by default and shown to others only if you choose to
  • Profile description/bio
  • Profile photos and cover photo

2.2 Content you create

  • Messages sent in event group chats and direct messages to other users
  • Voice messages (requires microphone access)
  • Photos attached to chats or events
  • Reviews given to or received from other participants; on the reviewed person's profile they appear only as an average, without the reviewer's name
  • Votes you give for other participants' badges
  • Reports submitted about other users (reason, description)
  • Feedback messages you send us, including attached photos, videos, or PDF files
  • The text you search for when choosing a place or route on the map

2.3 Your location — a special note

Location is a category of data we treat with special care, because it can reveal sensitive information about you.

  • Sharing your location is optional and requires your explicit consent, turned on separately for each share — either with a specific friend or with all participants of an event.
  • Each share has a limited duration that you set; it stops automatically when it expires. There is no permanent/unlimited location sharing.
  • While a share is active, we store: your current latitude and longitude, altitude, GPS accuracy, and your phone's battery percentage (so the people you share with can judge how recent and reliable the position is).
  • We only keep your last known position — we do not build a permanent history of your movements. Once you stop sharing or a share expires, your position is no longer visible to other users.
  • On iOS and Android, the app may request "Always" location permission, so an active share keeps working while your phone is locked or the app is in the background. You can decline this and grant "while using the app" access only — in that case, background sharing will not work.
  • You can turn off location sharing at any time, from within the app or from your phone's settings.

2.4 Notifications

  • A technical token (FCM) used to send you push notifications
  • Your notification preferences (which types of notifications you want to receive)
  • Time windows during which you don't want to receive notifications ("quiet hours")

2.5 Social and activity data

  • Your friends list and sent/received friend requests
  • Events you join, organize, or mark as favorites
  • Badges, experience points (XP), and your level in the app
  • A log of your relevant actions in the app ("Your Activity"), visible only to you
  • Whether you are online and when you have read a message ("Seen"), visible to the people you talk to

2.6 Technical data

  • Data generated automatically by the app's operation (e.g. technical error logs, IP address, app version, and device type), to the extent needed for the proper functioning and security of the service
  • On the website, information kept in your browser (local storage), strictly needed to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences — see the Cookie Policy

2.7 Donations

  • If you choose to support the project with a donation, payment takes place on the Revolut page, not in the app. Your card details never reach us; we only see what Revolut sends us about the payment (usually the amount and the donor's display name).

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. To provide the app's features (hosting your data, sending push notifications, storing photos and voice messages), we work with third-party hosting, storage, and push-messaging providers, who process data on our behalf, strictly for the purposes described in this policy, under contracts requiring confidentiality and security obligations equivalent to our own. The main providers are:

  • Contabo (Germany) — the server running the app and the technical error log;
  • Supabase — the database, authentication (account and password), and storage of photos and voice messages;
  • Google Firebase and the Apple and Google notification services — delivering push notifications;
  • Mapy.com (Czech Republic) and Photon / OpenStreetMap (Germany) — maps and place search; they receive the search text, not your name or account;
  • MET Norway (Norway) — weather forecasts; it only receives the event's coordinates, no data about you;
  • Revolut — only for donations, if you choose to donate.

Data you voluntarily share (your position on the map, messages, reviews) is also visible to the other users you've granted access to (friends, participants in the same event) — this is not "sharing with third parties", it's the app's core functionality, which you explicitly chose to use.

5. Transfers outside Romania/the EU

Some of the third-party providers mentioned in section 4 may process or store data on servers located outside Romania or the European Economic Area (for example, the push notification services of Google, Apple, and Firebase, in the USA). In such cases, we ensure the transfer relies on appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission) or another mechanism recognized under data protection law.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile data: for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is deleted or anonymized immediately (see section 9).
  • Location: only your last known position, for as long as a share is active; deleted automatically when the share expires or is stopped.
  • Messages, reviews, and events: for as long as the related event or conversation exists, because they are also part of the other participants' history.
  • Photos, videos, and files in group chats: deleted automatically 7 days after the event ends (those sent after the end, 7 days after being sent). In private messages they remain until you delete them.
  • User reports and feedback: for as long as needed to investigate and resolve them.
  • Technical and error logs: at most 90 days.

When your account is deleted, your profile data is deleted and the content you created remains anonymized, except for data we are legally required to retain for a further period.

7. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or accidental disclosure (e.g. encrypted communication between the app and our servers, access controls on our infrastructure). No method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we are committed to continuously improving these measures.

8. Your rights

As a data subject under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • access — find out what data we hold about you;
  • rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your data, under the conditions of the law;
  • restriction of processing;
  • data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format;
  • object — to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, for processing based on consent (e.g. location sharing), without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
  • lodge a complaint with the Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), www.dataprotection.ro, if you believe your rights have been infringed.

9. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time directly in the app (Profile → Settings → "Delete account permanently") or, without the app, by email — see the dedicated page: HikeTag account deletion.

On deletion, your name, email, photos, description, year of birth, and gender are removed. Events, messages, and reviews you left remain visible to the other participants, but under the name "Deleted user", and can no longer be linked to you. Upcoming events you were organizing are cancelled, and your places at other people's events are released.

10. Minors

HikeTag is intended for people 16 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you learn that someone under 16 has created an account, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically, to reflect legal or functional changes to the app. We will display the "last updated" date at the top of the document and, for significant changes, notify you within the app.

12. Governing law

This policy is governed by Romanian and EU data protection law, primarily Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Law No. 190/2018.
