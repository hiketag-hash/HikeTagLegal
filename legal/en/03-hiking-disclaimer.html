2. Risks of mountain and outdoor activities

Hiking, mountain excursions, and outdoor activities involve inherent risks, which may include, without limitation:

  • rough, slippery, or unstable terrain;
  • weather conditions that can change suddenly (fog, rain, storms, snow, extreme temperatures);
  • exposure to altitude, cold, excessive heat, dehydration, or physical exhaustion;
  • areas with weak or no phone signal;
  • the possibility of getting lost off the trail;
  • injuries, including severe injuries, and in extreme cases, death.

Participating in any Activity posted through HikeTag is entirely at your own risk.

3. Your responsibility as a participant

Before joining any Activity, it is your responsibility to:

  • honestly assess whether your physical fitness and experience match the Activity's difficulty;
  • check the weather forecast and trail conditions before setting out;
  • equip yourself properly (gear, food, water, clothing suited to the season and altitude);
  • inform someone you trust of your plans, even if you use the app's location-sharing feature;
  • carry adequate medical/travel insurance, if warranted by the activity;
  • consult a doctor beforehand if you have medical conditions that could be affected by intense physical effort, altitude, or extreme temperatures;
  • follow the Organizer's instructions and applicable laws on protected natural areas.

4. Organizers' responsibility

Users who organize Activities must comply with the Event Organizer Regulations, including the obligation to provide accurate information about the route, difficulty, and requirements. Failure to do so may result in account suspension, as described in the Terms and Conditions.

5. Shared transport

If you use the shared-transport (carpooling) feature to reach an Activity, note that HikeTag only facilitates coordination between driver and passengers and is not a party to any transport arrangement — see section 6 of the Terms and Conditions.

6. Emergencies

HikeTag is not an emergency service and does not actively monitor participants' locations. In case of real danger or an accident, contact emergency services immediately by calling 112.

By participating in any Activity posted through HikeTag, you confirm that you understand and accept the risks described above and that you participate at your own responsibility. To the maximum extent permitted by Romanian law, HikeTag and its operator are not liable for accidents, injuries, deaths, property loss, or other damages arising from an Activity organized between users, except where the law does not permit such a limitation (e.g. harm caused by the gross negligence of the app operator itself, as opposed to Organizers or other users).

8. Additional safety recommendations

  • Never set out alone on an unfamiliar mountain trail without telling someone.
  • Turn on location sharing in the app when participating in an Activity, if you want friends or fellow participants to see your position.
  • Check your phone's battery level before setting out.
  • If in doubt about weather conditions or trail state, postpone or cancel the Activity.
