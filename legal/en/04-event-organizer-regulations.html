Event Organizer Regulations — HikeTag

Last updated: August 3, 2026

These Regulations are part of the HikeTag Terms and Conditions and apply to any user who creates and publishes an event in the app ("Organizer").

1. Who can organize an event

Any user with a valid account, aged 16 or older, may create an event on HikeTag, provided they comply with these Regulations, the Terms and Conditions, and the Hiking Disclaimer.

2. Accurate event information

As an Organizer, you agree to provide real, complete, and up-to-date information about the event, including (where applicable):

  • the route, estimated duration, and difficulty level;
  • equipment required to participate;
  • the maximum number of participants (capacity);
  • the meeting point and time;
  • whether the event involves accommodation or shared transport organized through the app;
  • any estimated cost participants will split outside the app (e.g. fuel, accommodation) — note that HikeTag does not process payments and is not a party to any financial arrangement between participants.

You are responsible for updating this information if it changes and for notifying participants as soon as possible.

3. Managing participants

  • You may accept or decline requests to join your event, applying objective, non-discriminatory criteria (not based on race, ethnicity, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or other criteria protected by law).
  • If you cancel the event or make significant changes (route, date, meeting point), you must notify all accepted participants as early as possible.
  • Treat all participants with respect and communicate through the channels provided by the app (the event's chat).

4. Shared transport (carpooling)

If you offer transport seats through the app's carpooling feature, as a driver:

  • you must hold a valid driving license and valid third-party (RCA) insurance for the vehicle used;
  • you must respect the vehicle's legal seating capacity and applicable traffic law;
  • you are solely responsible for the vehicle's technical condition and for driving safely.

HikeTag only facilitates coordination of seat requests between driver and passengers and does not verify documents, insurance, or the condition of vehicles used — see section 6 of the Terms and Conditions.

5. Accommodation

If your event includes accommodation details, you agree to publish accurate information about the location, capacity, and conditions of the accommodation. HikeTag is not a party to any booking or accommodation contract entered into between participants and accommodation providers.

6. Participant safety

As an Organizer, you have a heightened responsibility toward the participants of your event:

  • openly communicate the specific risks of the route or activity (see the Hiking Disclaimer);
  • do not overstate your own experience or ability to guide other participants if you lack the necessary training;
  • do not invite participants on a route whose difficulty does not match the information published;
  • stop or adapt the activity if you observe dangerous weather or terrain conditions.

Organizing an event through HikeTag does not make you a professional mountain guide or a licensed tour operator, and the app does not certify or guarantee your qualification to act as one.

7. Your participants' data

As an Organizer, you may see certain profile data of participants (name, photo, contact details within the event's chat), strictly for the purpose of properly organizing that event. You agree to:

  • use this data only for the purpose of organizing the event the person is participating in;
  • not share, sell, or use this data for other purposes (e.g. your own marketing, other events without the person's consent);
  • comply with the HikeTag Privacy Policy and applicable data protection law.

8. Non-compliance with these Regulations

Failure to comply with these Regulations may result, depending on severity, in removal of the event from the app, an account warning, temporary suspension, or permanent deletion of the Organizer's account, as described in section 5 of the Terms and Conditions.

9. Limitation of liability

The liability-limitation provisions in the Terms and Conditions and the Hiking Disclaimer apply equally to organizing events. The Organizer remains solely responsible to participants for how their event is organized and carried out.
