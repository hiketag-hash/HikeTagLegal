Cookie Policy — HikeTag

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. They are used to remember preferences, measure traffic, or enable essential features of the site. A similar technology is the browser's local storage, which we treat the same way.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the www.hiketag.ro website (including the web version of the app and the documents pages). The mobile app does not use browser cookies; it only keeps on your phone what is needed to keep you signed in, as described in the Privacy Policy.

3. What we use

The HikeTag website uses only strictly necessary storage. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, neither our own nor third-party ones.

What we store Why Consent required?
Your sign-in session So you stay signed in across pages and visits No (strictly necessary)
Your preferences (e.g. theme, filters, notices you closed) So the site looks the way you left it No (explicitly requested by you when using the feature)

Maps on the website are loaded from Mapy.com and OpenStreetMap; like any website, they receive your browser's IP address in order to send you the map images.

4. How you control your preferences

Because we only use strictly necessary storage, under Romanian Law No. 506/2004 no consent banner is required. If we ever add cookies that are not strictly necessary (e.g. analytics), we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

You can delete the data stored by the site at any time from your browser settings; you will be signed out and your preferences will be reset.

5. Relationship to the Privacy Policy

This policy complements the HikeTag Privacy Policy, which describes all data collected through the app and the website.
