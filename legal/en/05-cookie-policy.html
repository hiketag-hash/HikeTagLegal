1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. They are used to remember preferences, measure traffic, or enable essential site functionality.

*(Note: the HikeTag mobile app uses equivalent native mechanisms — such as local storage on your phone — to keep you signed in. These are not browser cookies and are described in the Privacy Policy, not in this document.)*

4. How you control your preferences

Once the website is live, on your first visit you will see a cookie-consent banner, letting you accept, decline, or customize non-essential cookie categories, as required by applicable Romanian law (Law No. 506/2004 on the processing of personal data and the protection of privacy in the electronic communications sector, as amended).

You can also control or delete cookies from your browser settings at any time.
